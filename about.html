About

Two people,
one practice.

Juan Carlos

Co-owner

I’m Juan. More than twenty years of digital media, multilingual work, and technical support sit behind the practice.

Translation, interpretation, voice, graphic design, audio engineering, and computer systems are the mix. Supporting translation teams across dozens of languages sharpened the unglamorous part: time, communication, and problems that need solving the same day. Design, banking, and IT came earlier, and left a habit of adapting to the client in front of me.

At J.P. Creative Hub I want the work to read clearly, look professional, and arrive intact, whether that is language, design, or a piece of multimedia.

Pang

Co-owner · Lead interpreter and translator

I’m Pang, an English–Hmong interpreter and translator, with nearly a decade of professional work and a much earlier start. I was interpreting for my community while I was still young.

The settings have been medical, legal, educational, social, and multimedia. Accuracy and cultural care are the point. Voiceover, proofreading, and audio engineering sit alongside the language work.

At J.P. Creative Hub I want people to leave a conversation, or a document, clearer than they arrived.
