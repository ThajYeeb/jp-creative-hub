History of the Hmong — Classroom Player Sandbox

Hidden class player. Students pick a language for subtitles and headphone audio.

On the laptop connected to the projector, create a room and load the video file. Students scan the QR code or type the room code, pick a language, and use headphones to hear a natural-speed interpretation. Phones can follow with subtitles and headphone audio even without the video file. Use Classroom speakers to keep a phone silent.
